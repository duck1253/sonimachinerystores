OUR LEGACY ·

Built in Kanpur. Trusted across generations.

Soni Machinery Stores is a trusted machinery and equipment dealer in Kanpur, serving customers since 1974. For over 50 years, we have built our reputation on fair business practices, dependable service, genuine guidance and long-standing relationships with generations of customers.
